# Projects

Code that goes with my research and coursework. The research itself is on the [Research](./research) page.

- [**Polar Ensemble Decoding**](<https://github.com/eirinnnnnn/POD>)

Parallel polar-style decoding of binary linear block codes (extended BCH, extended Golay): simulation code, the logged curves behind the figures of my ITW 2026 paper, and plotting scripts.

- [**Attacks on Lattice-Based Cryptosystems**](<./assets/pdf/kul_ca_project1_report.pdf>)

Two course projects in Magma for Computer Algebra in Cryptography at KU Leuven (2026 Spring). Reports: [project 1](./assets/pdf/kul_ca_project1_report.pdf), [project 2](./assets/pdf/kul_ca_project2_report.pdf).

- [**Portable DSP Devices**](<https://github.com/eirinnnnnn/DSPLAB_2025FALL>)

DSP Lab at NYCU (2025 Fall): ultrasonic sensing, ECG acquisition and processing, motion sensing, and lidar on an AIK-RA8D1 development board. Some [lab notes](./notes/DSPLab) are here too.

- [**Robust Beamforming Design**](<https://github.com/eirinnnnnn/robust_miso_doubleQT>)

A convex optimization-based problem solving exercise, from my summer at UC Davis.

- [**RS Decoding**](<https://github.com/eirinnnnnn/RS_decoding>)

Final project of CCC's ECC course (NTHU Spring2025). Decoding RS using polynomial interpolation.

- [**DDPM Denoising Modulated Signals**](<https://github.com/eirinnnnnn/ddpm_denoising_modulated_signal>)

Using DDPM as a denoiser in downlink receiver.
